Cost snapshot

Premium fit-out

AED 10M – 16M

  • Full coal exhaust + fire strategy
  • 4 table-delivery robots + POS/KDS
  • Rooftop lighting + indoor fountain

Ultra-premium

AED 16M – 28M

  • Heavier rooftop structure & FF&E
  • Higher-spec lighting & furniture
  • Expanded kitchen / brand detailing

Robotics package

AED 270k – 610k

  • 3 indoor + 1 rooftop robots
  • Docks, mapping, QR menus, training
  • Coal service remains staff-only

Key constraint

Coal ≠ robots

  • Negative-pressure coal room
  • Dedicated roof exhaust stacks
  • Civil Defense + landlord NOC critical
