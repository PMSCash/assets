Classic VIP features

Fountain displays, fireplaces, and pole stage — all images also live in the images/ folder.

Pay policy

Strictly electronic. No cash. No tipping — tip fields disabled on POS and tablets; staff (including VIP hostesses) cannot accept tip gifts. After the bill, guests may make an optional electronic donation to a designated house fund or partner charity only — never to an individual staffer.

Brand · EJL Lighthouse

Flashy pink & gold logo — tagged on every delivery robot, VIP plaque, and tray liner.

Concept atmospheres

One VIP with office and bedroom · fountain, fireplace, and pole · plus rooftop / indoor direction.

A-04 · One VIP unit (lounge · office · bedroom)

Single principal VIP: entertainment lounge (fountain, fireplace, pole, 3 TVs + VOD), private office, bedroom with ensuite, hostess station, and service pass. One e-locked vestibule — door stays closed during the booking.

A-05 · Emergency / seizure treatment room

On-site first-response room: treatment bed, oxygen, suction, AED, seizure kit, stretcher park, and EMS route. Stabilizes guests until ambulance transfer — DHA pathway if stocking meds. Adjacent to staff rest & changing (A-06).

A-06 · Staff resting & changing room (closets)

Workers-only BOH: rest sofas and recliners, women/men changing booths, lockable personal closets C01–C12, uniform closet, shoe rack, staff WC, and phone cubbies. Badge access · no robots · no guests.

A-07 · Rooftop VIP-2 (second VIP)

Open-air e-locked terrace: pergola, outdoor fireplace, weather TV, 8 seats, hostesses, R4 service pass. Replaces 8 general rooftop seats when booked. No bedroom/office (those stay on indoor VIP-1).

A-08 · Kitchen / BOH (prep · cook · dish · storage)

Staff-only kitchen with prep, cook line, dish, cold/dry stores, robot loading bay, and dumbwaiter to rooftop.

M-01 · MEP systems (coal · HVAC · fire)

Schematic: dedicated coal stacks, separate kitchen hood, pressure cascade, sprinklers, fail-safe VIP e-doors.

F-01 · FF&E schedule (quantities)

Indicative furniture and equipment counts for VIP-1, VIP-2, FOH, BOH, staff, and clinical rooms.

G-01 · Guest journey (entry → VIP → pay)

Host → escort → e-unlock → experience → robot/hostess service → electronic pay → optional donation → depart.

P-01 · Electronic payment (no tip · donations only)

Card / NFC / wallet only. Bill has no tip line. Optional donation after e-receipt goes to house fund or charity — never to staff.

A-01 · Indoor floor plan

Dining, fountain, massage, one VIP (lounge · office · bedroom), kitchen, robot bay, coal room, emergency room, staff rest & changing with closets.

A-02 · Rooftop floor plan

28 lounge seats, bar handoff, exhaust stacks, robot aisle.

R-01 · Robot routing & order flow

QR order → KDS → load bay → table delivery. Coal room is a hard no-go zone for robots.

A-03 · Building section

Indoor + rooftop cut, dedicated coal exhaust to roof stacks, fountain, robot aisle.
